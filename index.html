About Me

Technology meets purpose

I am an IT Officer, educator, GIS specialist and founder based in Blantyre, Malawi. With over five years of hands-on experience across IT infrastructure, spatial data management, database administration and creative digital production I bring a rare combination of technical depth and creative versatility to every project.

Currently pursuing a BSc in Management Information Systems at the Malawi University of Business and Applied Sciences I am building KataTech Solutions — a technology company focused on delivering smart digital solutions for Malawian businesses, institutions and agricultural communities.

From building GIS land demarcation maps with ArcMap and AutoCAD to developing full-stack web applications, data dashboards and branded design systems — I work at the intersection of technology and impact.
